Short biography

Sewon Hur is an Associate Professor at Yonsei University’s Graduate School of International Studies in Seoul. Before joining Yonsei in 2025, he spent eight years in the Federal Reserve System, most recently as a principal research economist at the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas, and was an assistant professor at the University of Pittsburgh. His research on inflation, sovereign debt, trade, climate policy, and inequality has appeared in journals including the American Economic Journal: Macroeconomics, the Journal of Monetary Economics, and the Journal of International Economics. He is an associate editor of Quantitative Economics and holds a Ph.D. in economics from the University of Minnesota.
